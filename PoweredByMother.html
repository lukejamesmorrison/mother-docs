Mother HMI

by Dext3r09
AutomationUser InterfaceHMI

MotherHMI turns the LCDs of your base into an old-school control room. It draws your factory as a schematic made of cells: pipes, labels, lamps, arrows and level bars that show what the blocks are doing, plus buttons, switches and sorter controls that you operate with a cursor. Everything is set up by pasting text into Custom Data: you don't need to know any programming.

by Malleus
Mother OSMother GUI

This is based on Dar Kovalord's Astraea Class Corvette (Cargo) V1i, a versatile workhorse of a platform. This new Auxiliary variant is designed to handle the resupply of various stations, as well as acting as a secondary mining support vessel. It features a small on-board refinery configured to process stone and scrap to minimize weight, three jump drives giving it an impressive unladen range, and two assemblers configured to automatically restock needed supplies. It is intended to complement the existing fleet by providing logistical support and enhancing operational efficiency.
